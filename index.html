    走法 How pieces move
    • 帅/将 King: one step orthogonally, stays inside the palace (九宫). The two kings may never face each other on an open file.
    • 仕/士 Advisor: one step diagonally, inside the palace.
    • 相/象 Elephant: two steps diagonally, cannot cross the river, blocked if the middle point is occupied (塞象眼).
    • 马 Horse: one step straight then one diagonal; blocked by a piece next to it (蹩马腿).
    • 车 Chariot: any distance in a straight line.
    • 炮 Cannon: moves like a chariot, but captures by jumping over exactly one piece.
    • 兵/卒 Soldier: one step forward; after crossing the river it may also step sideways.
    • Checkmate or leaving the opponent with no legal move (困毙) wins. 60 moves each without a capture is a draw.
